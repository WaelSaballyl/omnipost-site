OmniPost

Publish one video to every platform you choose, from one place.

OmniPost is a personal publishing tool. You add a video once, pick which social platforms it should go to, write a caption, and OmniPost uploads it to each of them through their official APIs. It is built and used by a single creator to publish their own content to their own accounts.

What it does

  • Upload a video once and publish it to several platforms in one action.
  • Write a different title and description per platform, or share one caption.
  • Schedule a post for a specific time instead of publishing immediately.
  • Track the status of every platform separately and retry the ones that failed.
  • Warn before publishing when a clip is longer or larger than a platform allows.

Supported platforms

YouTube TikTok Instagram Facebook

How your account is used

OmniPost connects to a platform only after you sign in on that platform's own page and approve access. It never sees or stores your password. The access it receives is used for one thing: uploading the videos you choose to your own account. Nothing is posted without you starting it.
