Privacy Policy

OmniPost · Last updated 7 September 2026

OmniPost is a personal publishing tool that runs on the user's own computer. It is not a hosted service. There is no OmniPost server, no OmniPost account, and no OmniPost database holding user data. This policy describes what the application does with information on the device it runs on.

1. Information the application handles

Access tokens

When you connect a social media account, that platform's own sign-in page asks you to approve access. The platform then returns an access token and a refresh token to the application. OmniPost stores these tokens in a local database file on your computer (data/omnipost.db) and uses them only to publish the videos you select to your own account on that platform.

Passwords

OmniPost never receives, requests, displays, or stores your social media passwords. Sign-in happens entirely on the platform's own website.

Videos and captions

Videos you add are copied to a folder on your computer (uploads/) and are sent only to the platforms you explicitly select for that post. Titles, descriptions, tags and scheduling times are stored in the same local database.

Account details read from platforms

After you connect an account, the application may read basic public details about it — for example the channel or profile name — so it can show you which account is connected. This is display information only.

2. What the application does not do

  • It does not send your data to the developer or to any analytics service.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone.
  • It does not read your messages, contacts, followers, or private content.
  • It does not publish anything you did not explicitly ask it to publish.
  • It does not run in the background when you have not started it.

3. Who your data is shared with

Only the social platform you chose for a given post. When you publish to YouTube, the video and its caption go to YouTube. When you publish to TikTok, they go to TikTok. Nothing is sent anywhere else. Each platform then handles that content under its own privacy policy.

4. Where data is stored and for how long

Everything stays on your own computer, in the application's folder, for as long as you keep it. There is no cloud storage and no backup service. You control retention completely.

5. Deleting your data

  • Disconnecting an account in the app's settings deletes its stored tokens.
  • Deleting a clip in the app removes the video file from your computer.
  • Deleting the data/ and uploads/ folders removes everything the application ever stored.
  • You can also revoke the application's access at any time from the security or connected-apps settings of the platform itself.

Because no data ever leaves your device, there is nothing for the developer to delete on your behalf.

6. Security

Tokens are stored locally and are protected by your operating system's own user account protections. The application listens only on 127.0.0.1, which is not reachable from the internet or from other machines on your network. Anyone with physical access to your computer and your user session could read the application's files, so use OmniPost only on a device you control.

7. Children

OmniPost is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the minimum age required by the social platforms it connects to.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.
